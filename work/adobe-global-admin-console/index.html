INTRODUCING ADOBE GLOBAL ADMIN CONSOLE

Global Admin Console allows Global Admins to assign, manage, and monitor their Adobe products and resources across the enterprise in a way that is reflective of their organizational structure and business operations.

Designers, PMs, and engineers work iteratively 

During the design process, we had 9 Customer Advisory Board (CAB) sessions (bi-weekly) with 14 enterprise customers (e.g. WPP, Publicis, Deloitte, Microsoft, etc.) for design feedback. Alpha and beta programs started with the 14 CAB customers for user validation before the product went alive.

UNDERSTAND THE USER

Large organizations want to manage Adobe Consoles in a both hierarchical and flexible way 

After interviewing several customers (including Microsoft, Omnicom, and Disney), we realized that companies with complex organizational structures want to understand how their various divisions and sub-organizations function as a whole. They want to segregate child organizations to manage product usage, privacy policies, or to just reduce workload for their centralized IT department. 

  • Single Edit
  • Column Edit

WHAT'S THE PROBLEM

Today, a large company can have up to hundreds of siloed Admin Consoles  

Large organizations that have multiple divisions or companies are required to use multiple, siloed Admin Console accounts to manage their users and licenses. Companies are also given the option to consolidate their accounts into a single Admin Console, but this does not provide the multi-tiered segregation and management of resources and users that large, global organizations need.

  • Single Edit
  • Column Edit

FOCUS OF THE GLOBAL CONSOLE

Large organizations with multiple divisions/companies

The focus of Global Admin Console is large centralized organizations. It gives high-level management of multiple admin consoles. Global admins can define the structure among all admin consoles, create/delete admin consoles, allocate product resources.

Jane - the Global Admin

• The global admin has higher permission than the system admin of an individual admin console. 
• Only global admin has access to the global console.
• The initial global admin is appointed Adobe agent during the on-boarding process.
• A global admin can appoint other global admins and system admins in the global console. 

WORKFLOW

Onboarding, Set up, Use, and Support

The workflows enable users to represent, visualize and operationalize their organizational structures on the Adobe platform. It provides high-level insights into org structure and usage to help our customers understand how Adobe is serving their organizations and make the best decision.
