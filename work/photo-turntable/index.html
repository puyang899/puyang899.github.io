MY ROLE

Designing the system in an iterative process of user research, ideation, prototyping, and testing. 

INTRODUCING PHOTO TURNTABLE

Photo Turntable is a hardware/software system that provides a tangible way to organize, view, and share digital photos. With a photo turntable, people can easily create their favorite albums, view and share digital photos with their family and friends within their home environment.

USER RESEARCH

Photo is evolved from physical to digital in a short amount of time. To understand how people organize, view, and share their digital photos, user research is conducted in several methods, including interviews, observation, task walkthroughs, and artifact analysis.

WHAT IS THE PROBLEM?

Nowadays, people take photos by the camera, cellphone, and tablet; they take photos when they are traveling, working, and eating; they share photos easily over distance by Flickr, Facebook, and Instagram, etc. 

However, People rarely review their digital photos. 
Compared with distance photo sharing, there are fewer opportunities for people to view and share digital photos within the home environment.

PROTOTYPE

Behavior and appearance prototypes are developed to evaluate the concepts. For the software, clickable wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes are used. For the turntable product, Arduino is used to demonstrating the concept. 

  • Single Edit
  • Column Edit

USER EVALUATION

By using behavior and appearance prototypes, 24 user evaluation sessions in total are conducted. The goal is to validate the content and interaction of the system and get feedback about what needs to be done next. The results showed that the system was easy to understand and use, and people found the product to provide an enjoyable experience.
